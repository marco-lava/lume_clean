# Lumé — Design
Italian single-page beauty studio demo. Editorial luxury, warm ivory, botanical elegance.
## Palette
Ivory #f8f6f0; sage #788674; olive #354536; blush #eee3dc; gold #b39961. CSS variables and Tailwind custom theme.
## Typography
Self-hosted and preloaded Cormorant Garamond for display, Montserrat for body. Italic serif accents.
## Layout
1240px content, spacious split hero with real Pexels photography, fine rules and pill CTAs. Editorial service split and white testimonial cards on sage-gray. Mobile stacked sections and no dotted leaders.
## Flows
Navigation anchors; accessible animated price accordion; service selection populates calendar/form; month navigation, date and time selection; client-side validated appointment simulation with explicit no-send confirmation. Mobile menu.
## Scope
Web only. Homepage, booking component, CSS, metadata, local fonts and photos. No backend routes or database tables needed for approved simulation. Preserve analytics and platform components. Prices, reviews and address clearly marked as demo.
